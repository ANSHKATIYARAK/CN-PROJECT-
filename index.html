Hierarchical 5-Area Campus Architecture

How partitioning the 372-acre VIT Vellore campus isolates failure domains, prevents SPF CPU thrashing, and delivers uninterrupted connectivity for 40,000+ students.

Area 0 • Backbone Core

Centre for Technical Support (CTS DC)

The central high-speed optical spine linking all campus sectors. Terminates all inter-area optical trunks with dual-redundant 100 Gbps core links.

  • Physical Nodes: CTS Core 1 & CTS Core 2
  • Subnet Prefix: 10.0.0.0/24 (Point-to-Point /30 links)
  • Role: Full LSDB synchronization; zero summarization inside Area 0.
Area 10 • Academic Core

SJT, Tech Tower, MB & SMV Hexagon

Serves 18,500 daily engineering students across 12 floors of Silver Jubilee Tower, computing labs in Technology Tower, and multidisciplinary classrooms.

  • Boundary Gateways: SJT-ABR (10.10.0.1) & TT-ABR (10.10.64.1)
  • Subnets: 10.10.0.0/18 (SJT) & 10.10.64.0/18 (TT)
  • Summarization: Compressed into 10.10.0.0/16 at Area Border Routers.
Area 20 • Residential Hostels

35+ Men's & Women's Hostel Blocks

Houses 32,500 resident students across Men's Hostels (Blocks A to T) and Women's Hostels (Blocks A to H). High-frequency Wi-Fi flaps are completely isolated inside Area 20.

  • Boundary Gateways: MH-ABR (10.20.0.1) & WH-ABR (10.20.128.1)
  • Subnets: 10.20.0.0/17 (Men's) & 10.20.128.0/17 (Women's)
  • Summarization: Aggregated into 10.20.0.0/16 into the core backbone.
Area 30 • Central Admin (Totally Stubby)

Gandhi Block Administration & Finance

Houses high-security administrative, registrar, and finance servers. Totally Stubby Area shielding blocks all external and inter-area LSAs to prevent reconnaissance attacks.

  • Boundary Gateway: Admin-ABR (10.30.0.1)
  • Subnet Prefix: 10.30.0.0/16 (Private Administrative VLANs)
  • Shielding: Blocks Type-3/4/5 LSAs; installs a synthesized 0.0.0.0/0 default route.
Area 40 • Research & Incubation (NSSA)

Pearl Research Park (PRP 7 Floors)

Houses 4,800 researchers, supercomputing clusters, and AI incubators. Allows external route redistribution via Type-7 LSAs translated to Type-5 at PRP-ABR.

  • Boundary Gateway: PRP-ABR (10.40.0.1)
  • Subnet Prefix: 10.40.0.0/16 (Research Clouds & HPC Labs)
  • NSSA Policy: Converts local Type-7 LSAs into Type-5 for campus-wide reachability.

OSPF Protocol Mechanics & Packet Structures

Standard RFC 2328 link-state exchange: Packet header bit-fields, 5 packet types, and 8-state neighbor finite state machine.

Standard 24-Byte OSPF Packet Header

Every OSPF packet sent across the campus uses this fixed 24-byte header over IP Protocol 89:

Version (8b)0x02
Type (8b)1 to 5
Length (16b)Variable
Router ID (32 bits)IPv4 Address (e.g. 10.0.0.1)
Area ID (32 bits)0.0.0.0, 0.0.0.10, 0.0.0.20, ...
Checksum (16b)Fletcher
AuType (16b)0x0002 (MD5)
Authentication (64b)Key ID, Sequence, Digest Length

8-State Neighbor Finite State Machine (FSM)

1Down: Initial state; no Hello received.
2Init: Hello received; local RID not yet seen by peer.
32-Way: Bidirectional link verified; DR/BDR elected.
4ExStart: Master/Slave relationship negotiated.
5Exchange: Database Description (DBD) summaries exchanged.
6Loading: Link-State Requests (LSR) and Updates (LSU) sent.
7Full: LSDBs synchronized; optimal routing active.
